The Fed's hawkish turn is doing real damage now, and the selling is no longer contained to the speculative corners. The unprofitable tech trade has bled for months, but this week the knife turned on the megacaps — the likes of Nvidia and Tesla are breaking technical levels and the dip-buyers who rescued every pullback last year are simply absent. I watch the two-year make higher highs every morning as the market prices a more aggressive hiking path, and every tick takes another leg out of duration-heavy growth. What strikes me most is the absence of panic: no capitulation volume, no VIX above 30, just a relentless grind that bleeds out anyone who bought the last dip. Managers are cutting winners to raise cash because their losers are unsellable. The buy-every-correction playbook is dead, and I suspect more air comes out before this finds a floor.
