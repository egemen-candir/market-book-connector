We woke up to a different country. Sterling is down eight big figures, trading at levels not seen since the mid-eighties, and the board is a mess — every time a count comes in from a Leave area the pound ticks lower. European bank shares are in freefall: RBS is down more than a third, Barclays isn't much better, and the Italian and Spanish banks are limit-down. The FTSE opened about eight percent lower and has clawed back half of that, which tells you equities at least see this as a sterling and bank problem first. Gold is up better than a hundred dollars. Carney has been on the wires promising £250 billion of support if needed, and that has steadied rates somewhat. A vote that was supposed to be a coin flip is being traded as a regime change.
