The volatility blowup everyone half-expected has arrived. VIX nearly doubled yesterday, closing above 37 after an intraday spike past 50 — the highest since 2015 — and the overnight session is uglier still, with Cboe halting VIX futures trading at limit-up. The short-vol complex is being annihilated: XIV is indicated down something like ninety percent and was halted ahead of what everyone assumes will be a termination, SVXY is down in the sixties, and that leverage is unwinding into a cash market already gapping lower — S&P futures are off another three percent after yesterday's hundred-point rout. A year of sub-11 VIX prints built the biggest short-vol trade in history, and tonight it is being force-fed out the door. Long vol from here is a momentum trade, not a hedge, and nobody knows where the unwind ends.
