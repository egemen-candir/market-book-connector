I traded through 2008 and I have never seen anything like this stretch. Circuit breakers have tripped at the open more than once this week, the S&P is down roughly thirty percent in a month, and the strangest part is what is being sold: ten-year yields have backed up toward 1.2% from record lows, gold has been dumped, and everyone is scrambling for dollar cash — funds, dealers, even sovereign accounts. The Fed cut to zero and launched massive asset purchases, swap lines with other central banks just got cheaper and longer, and Congress is still haggling, but the bid never shows up where it should. Treasury futures bid-ask is embarrassing, money is hiding in bills and repo, and liquidity, not solvency, is the immediate enemy.
