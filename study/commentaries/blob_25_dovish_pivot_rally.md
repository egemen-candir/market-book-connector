Christmas came early this year, and the Fed delivered it. The dot plot last week signaled roughly seventy-five basis points of cuts for 2024 and Powell, unprompted, acknowledged the committee is already discussing them — that was the switch. The ten-year, above five percent in October, is back near 3.90 and still grinding lower; two-year yields fell hard on the meeting. Equities have rallied through the chop into year-end, the S&P within reach of its all-time high, with cyclicals and small caps finally taking the baton from the megacaps. Gold printed record highs above 2,100, bitcoin pushed through 40,000, and investment-grade and high-yield issuance is flooding out as borrowers lock in the pivot. Everything is up at once — stocks, bonds, commodities, crypto — which tells you this is a rates story, not an earnings one. Bears are capitulating into the tape, and positioning says January starts crowded.
