It is getting harder to ignore how narrow this tape has become. Nvidia alone is up more than 150% this year after another blowout quarter, and just a handful of AI-adjacent names — Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta — now account for a record share of the S&P 500, north of 30% by most counts. Strip them out and the average stock has gone essentially nowhere for months; the equal-weight index trails the cap-weighted version by several percentage points, and breadth measures sit at their weakest levels since the dot-com era. Clients keep asking whether this is 1999 all over again. I don't know the answer, but passive flows keep buying the biggest weights regardless, and until that machine breaks, fighting the concentration trade has proven an expensive business.
