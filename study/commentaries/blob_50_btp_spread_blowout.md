Ten-year BTPs are above three per cent and the spread to Bunds has blown out toward three hundred basis points, the fastest widening since the euro crisis, and it is all politics. The coalition's insistence on Paolo Savona at the economy ministry, with his history of plan B currency talk, has investors pricing genuine redenomination risk into Italian assets. Two-year yields have gone from a rounding error to north of two and a half per cent in days, Italian bank stocks are being sold hard and the FTSE MIB is the worst major index in Europe. Bunds are bid as the safe haven, so the spread overshoots whatever the true fundamental story is. Foreign holders are cutting Italian exposure at any price and those who cannot are hedging, because until Rome clarifies its commitment to the euro every rally in BTPs is a trade, not a home.
