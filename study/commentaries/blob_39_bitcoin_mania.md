Bitcoin is up better than sixty percent in two weeks and approaching $20,000, and the mania is no longer confined to trading desks. Coinbase has added hundreds of thousands of accounts in days, exchanges keep buckling under signups, and my dentist is asking about cold storage. The Cboe futures debut on Sunday was orderly enough to be a story in itself, and the CME launch a week from now will give institutions their first real access. Every taxi driver and holiday party has a bitcoin opinion; nobody asks about earnings or cash flow because there are none. I keep telling anyone who will listen that an asset that goes vertical like this can go vertical the other way just as fast, but momentum like this makes converts of everyone. Parabolic charts end, though none of us know when.
