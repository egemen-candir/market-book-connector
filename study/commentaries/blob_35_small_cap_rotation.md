One inflation print and the market map has been redrawn. The cool CPI set off a rotation I have rarely seen in violence: small caps gapped and ran — the Russell up something like ten percent in days — while the mega-cap leaders are sold hard. For months the story was concentration, AI winners, fewer stocks carrying the market, and suddenly the laggards are the trade and everyone forced into megacaps for liquidity is reversing. Whether it lasts is the debate on every call I take. The bull case: an easing cycle flatters small caps with their floating-rate debt and cyclical exposure. The bear case: one print does not make a trend, and the squeeze includes fast money chasing underperformance that leaves as fast as it came. The megacap selloff is orderly — reallocation, not de-risking. If small caps hold for two more weeks, the regime has genuinely changed.
