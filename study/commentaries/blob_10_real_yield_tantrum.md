The bond market is doing the Fed's tightening for it. Ten-year yields are pressing 1.55% after starting the year under 1%, and the snap higher in real yields from around minus one to nearly minus 0.7% has finally broken something: the Nasdaq just had its worst day since October, and the high-multiple growth names that defined last year are taking the brunt while banks, energy, and beaten-down value finally catch a persistent bid. Everyone calls it the reflation trade — vaccines rolling out, data surprising higher, stimulus hopes building — but the speed of the move is the story. Powell testified this week that policy stays patient for a long time, and the Fed is visibly unbothered. I expect the rotation to stay violent until long yields find a level that breaks something bigger.
