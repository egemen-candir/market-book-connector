That CPI print was a gut punch. Nobody had 8.6 for the headline and the bond market did not waste a second: the ten-year backed up violently and the front end prices a seventy-five basis point hike this month as a live option rather than a tail. Stocks and bonds are down together — the configuration that hurts every balanced portfolio — and there is nowhere to hide; even gold is slipping as real yields rip. The Nasdaq is down hard because higher discount rates hurt long-duration assets most, but the selling is broad. The Fed has been explicit about needing deceleration, and this print guarantees a hawkish meeting. The tragedy is that positioning had finally built a thawing narrative — peak inflation, peak hawkishness — and the data just torched it. Until the two-year rolls over, equities are fighting gravity.
