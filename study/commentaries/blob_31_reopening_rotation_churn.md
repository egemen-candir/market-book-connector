The whipsaw is exhausting the machinery. One week the reopening trades — banks, energy, travel, small caps — lead with conviction; the next, money floods back into the stay-at-home megacaps and everything cyclical gets marked down. Through it all the ten-year chops sideways, giving neither side a macro anchor, so rotation, not direction, is the only game in town. Allocators genuinely do not know whether growth survives, so they trade ranges rather than build positions. Momentum desks get whipped because leadership flips before factor signals adjust. The average stock goes nowhere while the tape feels busy — classic churn, high volume, low persistence. My worry is that this regime bleeds performance out of everyone until someone big capitulates to a trend just as it exhausts. I am cutting size and widening stops: being right about the theme is no longer enough — you must also be right about the week.
