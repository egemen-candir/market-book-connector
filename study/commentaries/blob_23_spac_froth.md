Everything speculative is bid and I mean everything. Roughly three hundred blank-check SPACs have IPO'd this year, pulling in north of ninety billion dollars, and sponsor track records now matter more than the target itself — shares pop on the announcement, hold through the merger, and the deal machine feeds on itself. Chamath launches a new vehicle and it's oversubscribed in hours. Profitless software names trade at twenty times revenue, unprofitable EV companies that went public via reverse mergers are worth tens of billions pre-production, and Coinbase just listed directly at a hundred billion dollars. Retail is the marginal buyer by a mile — record account openings, options volumes, Reddit threads, Cathie Wood as folk hero. My institutional book looks stodgy next to this. Spreads on new issues are trivial, quality is an afterthought, and anyone pointing at valuations has been wrong for a year. The tape doesn't punish anything.
