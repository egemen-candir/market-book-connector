Markets here have never fallen this hard, this fast. After Sunday's surprise primary handed Alberto Fernández a fifteen-point victory over President Macri, the peso collapsed as much as thirty percent, trading past sixty to the dollar before paring losses, and sovereign bonds are down twenty cents with yields careering toward fifty percent. The Merval's plunge is among the worst single-day drops for any index in years, far uglier in dollars. Country risk has spiked above seventeen hundred basis points, pricing in a restructuring of the record IMF program Macri took and Fernández called usurious on the campaign trail. The central bank burned reserves today, auctioning dollars into a void, and still the selling came. I am watching banks and energy names — Grupo Galicia, YPF, Pampa — trade like lottery tickets, because nobody knows what policy looks like under a Fernández government. The unknown is the entire trade.
