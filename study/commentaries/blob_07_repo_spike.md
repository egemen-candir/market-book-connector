Repo went vertical this week: overnight funds printed near ten percent intraday and the effective fed funds rate broke above the target range, something I have not seen since the crisis-era plumbing debates. The Fed responded with overnight and term repo operations, lending reserves against Treasuries and MBS — the first such injections in years. The proximate culprits are corporate tax dates and a large Treasury settlement draining cash, but underneath it feels structural: bill supply has ballooned, reserves look scarcer than the Fed assumed, and quarter-end is bearing down. Funding desks are nervous, bid-ask in bills is wide, and I would not be surprised if the central bank keeps supplying liquidity through month-end while it studies the reserve question.
