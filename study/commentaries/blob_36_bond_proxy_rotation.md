The bond market keeps making new lows in yield and equity investors keep running up the wall of negative carry. Ten-year Treasuries sit near 1.40% and much of the German curve is underwater, so the hunt for income has turned ferocious. Utilities are at all-time highs, telecoms and staples aren't far behind, and I watch clients pay twenty times earnings for Procter & Gamble and Coca-Cola simply because the dividend out-yields the bond. McDonald's now yields more than the ten-year. That this passes for a valuation argument tells you where we are. Everyone knows bond proxies are stretched — everyone says they can't afford to fight the flows. Crowded trades feel comfortable right up until they don't, and this one feels very crowded.
