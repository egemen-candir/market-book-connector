The two-ten spread has gone negative for the first time since 2007, and the tape is not letting me look away. Ten-years sit near 1.58% with the long bond hovering under 2% for the first time ever, and every front page carries a recession headline. This is a trade-war curve, not a Fed one: Beijing let the yuan slide through seven, another round of tariffs lands this weekend, and the market has concluded the expansion is running out of runway. Stocks gapped lower yesterday and could not recover, while September cut pricing firmed up after July's insurance move. I keep telling clients the inversion itself predicts little on any usable clock, but positioning is long duration, sentiment is fragile, and right now the headlines are winning.
