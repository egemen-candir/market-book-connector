Another week, another string of record highs that feels like nothing. The S&P grinds up a few tenths of a percent a day, and the one modest dip we saw was bought back within hours. The VIX printed below ten again — a record low — and my desk conversation has drifted to what could possibly wake this market up. Earnings are beating, global growth looks synchronized, and every pullback gets absorbed instantly by dip-buyers and buyback programs. Volatility sellers are having the run of their careers; the short-vol complex keeps minting money and, not coincidentally, keeps absorbing any risk that dares show up. I can't argue with the trend, and there is no macro catalyst on the calendar, but positioning this one-sided makes me uneasy. Complacency, not valuation, is the risk I'm watching. Until something breaks the pattern, though, staying long remains the only trade that pays.
