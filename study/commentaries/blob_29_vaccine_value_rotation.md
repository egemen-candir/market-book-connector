I have never seen anything quite like Monday's session. Pfizer and BioNTech's interim readout — ninety percent efficacy, better than most dared hope — triggered what may be the sharpest single-day rotation from growth to value ever recorded. The Russell 2000 jumped more than six percent to a record, energy and financial names gapped higher across the board, and the Dow had one of its best sessions of the year. Meanwhile everything that won the lockdown trade got obliterated: the work-from-home complex fell double digits intraday, and the Nasdaq actually closed red on a day the broader market surged. Fund managers spent the day scrambling into cyclicals, small caps, and anything priced for a permanent recession. Whether this is a durable regime change or a violent trade around the ruins of the old one, nobody can say yet. The ferocity is the story.
