I have covered crypto through Mt. Gox and this feels worse. FTX, a top-three exchange just days ago, has halted withdrawals and its putative rescuer is gone: Binance walked away from its letter of intent after reviewing the books, citing mishandled customer funds. FTT, the exchange token that anchored Alameda Research's balance sheet, is down roughly eighty percent this week to around three dollars. Bitcoin has broken under seventeen thousand for the first time since 2020; ether is below twelve hundred. Every leveraged lender — Genesis, BlockFi, the rest — is now under a microscope, with withdrawal runs the obvious next domino. Stablecoins are bending, funding is deeply negative, and customers are pulling whatever they can to self-custody. Nobody wants to be the next exchange to discover its assets aren't there. This is an extinction event in real time.
