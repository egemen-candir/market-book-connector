The air has come out of the mega-caps fast. After a parabolic summer, the big tech names have dropped double digits in a handful of sessions while the broader index barely budges — one of the most violent rotations I remember, and almost nobody's portfolio is built for it. Everything crowded is unwinding together: the popular ETFs, the hedge fund favorites, the retail darlings. Options tell the story too; call volumes went vertical and now dealers unwind that gamma on the way down, exaggerating every move. What fascinates me is the divergence — cyclicals, financials, and energy quietly working higher, so this is not risk-off but a controlled demolition of one trade funding another. Fundamentals have not changed in a week, but the marginal buyer is gone and momentum funds are mechanical sellers. The rest of the market holding firm is the tell: an intra-market transfer, not a bear phase.
