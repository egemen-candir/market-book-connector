The Evergrande headlines have finally broken through. Equities gapped lower at the open and there is real panic-selling in anything levered to Chinese property — iron ore, Australian miners, the Hong Kong developers obliterated. The question every desk is asking is whether Beijing lets it fail or forces an orderly restructuring, and nobody has a good answer. Asian high-yield spreads have blown out and the nervous energy feels like a proper risk-off day rather than a wobble. My sense is direct Western exposure is limited, but that is exactly what people said about Turkey in 2018, and momentum is a brutal teacher. The VIX is bid, Treasuries are catching flows, and positioning looks nervous rather than capitulatory — which tells me the de-risking has further to run until the authorities offer clarity. Contagion is a story, not yet a fact, but the market is starting to price it as one.
