Shanghai was a horror show again — the circuit breakers got scrapped after the first week, but the selling hasn't stopped, and the composite is back under 3,000 for the first time in more than a year. Oil is what's breaking everything now: WTI has traded under $30 and touched the mid-$20s overnight, and the energy complex is dragging credit and the banks down with it. U.S. equities have given back the entire bounce from Wednesday, and the S&P is now down around ten percent for the year — three weeks in. Nobody wants to be the last one out of the risk trade; even the dip-buyers from December have gone quiet. Gold is catching a real bid, and pricing of a March Fed hike has essentially gone to zero. This feels like de-risking for its own sake.
