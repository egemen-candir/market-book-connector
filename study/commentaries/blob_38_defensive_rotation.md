Beneath the choppy tape the story is rotation, and it is not subtle. Money is leaving anything with a high beta: the FANG complex keeps getting sold into every bounce, semiconductors can't hold a bid, and the momentum unwind from October has simply continued under a different name. Meanwhile low-volatility ETFs are taking in assets at a pace I haven't seen since February, utilities have quietly outperformed the S&P by several points this month, and the dividend aristocrats are acting like a safe haven. Clients say they are not de-risking, they are re-pricing duration into an index that is pricing in December hikes and a trade war with no exit. Whether this is defensive positioning or the start of something worse, the tape doesn't say. It just says: own the bond proxies, sell the winners.
