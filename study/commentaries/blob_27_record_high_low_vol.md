Another week, another set of record highs, and still the market refuses to offer a decent entry point. The index has closed at an all-time high in something like a third of the sessions since Labor Day, realized volatility has collapsed into the low single digits, and the VIX sits listlessly in the mid-teens. Every dip gets bought within a day, sometimes within the hour. Yet the scepticism in my inbox has not gone away — strategists keep pointing at narrow leadership, rich multiples, and the enormous AI spending bills funding the move, and asking what happens if any leg of it stumbles. For now the answer appears to be nothing. Positioning looks more hedged than euphoric, and complacency is easier to allege than to measure. Calm markets breed uneasy commentary, and this one is very calm indeed.
