I am writing with the financial system in genuine disarray. Silicon Valley Bank is gone — a full deposit run in forty-eight hours, regulators stepped in Friday, and every regional bank chart looks like a cliff. Signature is under scrutiny and the shorts are working down the list of banks with big uninsured deposit bases and unrealized securities losses. The two-year Treasury is extraordinary: the biggest weekly moves in decades as the market prices cuts within months, because a banking crisis does the Fed's tightening for it. Conversations shifted from inflation to contagion in days, and clients now ask whether deposits are safe above the limit — the psychological threshold has been crossed. Megabanks catch a bid as the strong hands; regionals are unsavable on momentum alone. Broken deposit trust does not return on a press release.
