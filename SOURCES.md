# Sources

The Market Book catalogue summarises and interprets published research. The records are
the project's own syntheses; citing a paper does not imply endorsement by its authors.
The papers are not covered by this repository's licences. Known errors in the catalogue
text, including author credits, are listed in
[connector/catalog/CATALOGUE_ERRATA.md](connector/catalog/CATALOGUE_ERRATA.md).

## Primary sources

These 14 works account for all 31 catalogue entries. Published citations and the
versions actually used are distinguished below. Verification status concerns the
bibliography, not the correctness of the catalogue's interpretations; manuscript
version dates are transcribed from the retained covers.
